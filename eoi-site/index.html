01 · The assignment

A case file that follows the survivor

Kesi Yangu Digital strengthens community-led monitoring of reported S/GBV cases. Survivors report over SMS; authorized YA4C staff verify and manage cases in a secure dashboard; one monitoring record follows each case across police, health, psychosocial, legal and justice pathways — without replacing them — and becomes anonymized evidence for engaging duty bearers.

01

Safeguarding first

Kenya DPA 2019, data minimization and trauma-informed design shape the data model and every flow — never bolted on after.

02

SMS for everyone

The survivor channel is SMS: no smartphone or connectivity required. The web side stays usable by non-technical staff, on and offline.

03

Proven foundations

Adapt a shipped SGBV case-management system instead of re-deriving architecture — so a pilot is live in three months, not a year.

02 · The survivor experience

Reporting over SMS, end to end

Survivors text a keyword to a shortcode. A numbered menu walks them through a short, trauma-informed intake — no PII beyond age group and gender — then issues a case ID. Texting STATUS <id> returns where the case sits in the referral pathway.

For survivors

Zero-download, works on any phone and poor network. Registration + status retrieval via a unique case identifier.

For staff

The same case appears in full in the secure dashboard for verification and referral follow-up.

05 · Approach

Four phases, secure by design

A phased delivery that lands a deployable pilot in Month 3, with safeguarding and data protection governing every step.

PHASE 0

Mobilization pre-Month 1

Confirm user journey, requirements, data flows and safeguarding with YA4C; agree referral mappings and success criteria.

PHASE 1

Foundation & SMS Month 1

Architecture and functional spec; survivor SMS intake and case-ID retrieval on a local gateway; database, hosting, baseline security.

PHASE 2

Dashboard & data Month 2

Secure case-management dashboard; referral follow-up; anonymized analytics; role-based access, encryption, backups, audit.

PHASE 3

Test, deploy, handover Month 3

Functional, security and usability testing; UAT and defect resolution; training, documentation, handover and initial support.

Principles

Trauma-informed UX · data minimization · open source where reliable · offline resilience · human oversight of any AI recommendation.

Technology

Africa's Talking SMS gateway · PostgreSQL (Supabase) with row-level security · React PWA dashboard · FHIR R4 for health-system linkage.
